EY point-to-point

Fare = max(minimum, billable minutes × minute rate + kilometres × km rate). The grace period is deducted once. No P2P night charge.

EY rental packages

The CSV Package fare selects the starting package. Extra time is charged per minute after package hours and one grace period.

Upgrade 4h/40km to 6h/60km after 5 hours; upgrade to 8h/80km after 7 hours or 70 km. Exactly at a threshold, the current package's extra charges still apply.

Night charges apply to rentals only when the trip overlaps the night window. Rental kilometres use the same whole-kilometre treatment as PWC.
